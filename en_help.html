Karaborsa Simülasyonu — Help

Welcome! Karaborsa Simülasyonu ("Black Market Simulation") is a fully fictional trading and business-management game. You buy and sell goods on a fluctuating black market, open companies for steady profit and credit, invest in real estate and warehouse space, hire employees, manage risk with an informant, and try to avoid the police — all while building the biggest fortune you can. This page is your quick reference for how everything works.

Buying & Selling

The heart of the game is the product list: every item has a price that changes from day to day, sometimes gently, sometimes sharply after a random event. Buy low, hold onto stock, and sell high. Watch the category of each product (press D to hear it) — some categories carry more risk (and more police attention) than others.

The price shown in the list is the price you pay when buying. When you sell, a 5% commission is deducted from the market price, so a quick buy-and-sell on the same day always costs you a little — you need a real price move to come out ahead.

Inventory limit: the goods in your main inventory can be worth at most 100,000 in total (at current market prices). When you reach the limit you cannot buy more until you sell some goods or move them into a land warehouse (see below). Goods you already own are never taken away, even if a price jump pushes you above the limit.

Daily Events

Each time a new day starts, a few random events may happen: price shocks for a whole category, cash windfalls or losses, goods found or lost, and land price changes. Most events affect a small percentage of your wealth, and bad news is as common as good news, so don't count on luck to make you rich.

Companies

A company is a business you can open in a city of your choice. Companies cost money to found and have a daily upkeep, but they earn a profit every day and build up a credit score. A better credit score and steady income unlock bank loans on better terms. Miss the upkeep payment and the company goes under. You can own several companies at once.

Land

Real estate is a slower, steadier way to grow your wealth. Land prices drift over time and can jump after certain events, and you can also take out a loan using land you own as collateral (land with an unpaid loan cannot be sold). Open F6 for the full land management screen.

Land Warehouse

Every piece of land you own adds to a shared warehouse. Its capacity is half of the price you paid for each land, added together, and it is measured in the market value of the stored goods. So a plot you bought for 50,000 lets you store goods worth up to 25,000.

  • Open F6 and choose the Warehouse button. Pick a product, enter a quantity, and choose Store in Warehouse or Take from Warehouse. There are also buttons to store as much as fits and to take everything back.
  • Goods in the warehouse do not appear in your inventory and do not count toward the inventory limit. They stay there until you take them out.
  • Taking goods out is always allowed, even if your inventory is full, so your goods are never stuck.
  • The police still notice stored illegal goods, but only at half the weight of goods in your inventory. Hiding everything in the warehouse will not make you invisible.
  • You cannot sell a land if the remaining warehouse capacity would be too small for the goods stored in it. Take some goods out first. If the bank takes a land because of a missed loan payment, the capacity drops in the same way; you can still take your goods out, but you can't store more until you are under the new capacity.
  • You need at least one land before you can use the warehouse.

Bank Loans

Short on cash? If you own a company, the bank will lend you money based on your companies' income and credit score. The better your score, the more you can borrow and the lower the interest. Loans are repaid in installments, one every 30 days. If you cannot pay an installment, the loan defaults, so only borrow what you can realistically pay back.

Employees

Hire people and send them to a city (one employee per city). Each employee earns cash for you every day, and earns a little more as they gain experience. You pay a one-time hiring fee, and it rises slightly with every employee you already have, so a huge network gets expensive. Every 30 days each employee is paid a salary; if you can't pay, they leave. Employees are managed from the Employee screen (F7).

Informants & the Police

The riskier your activity, the more "police heat" you build up. Hiring an informant costs money and warns you when the police might be coming the next day, so you can dump your stock or lay low. The warning is a prediction, not a guarantee: sometimes the informant is wrong. If your heat gets too high and you're caught, you'll end up in jail for a number of days — during which your businesses and loans keep running without you, so it pays to stay prepared.

Auction House

Press F8 to open the Auction House. This is a live, real-time auction: the moment you open it, a random item (an antique, a piece of jewelry, a classic car, and more) goes up with an opening announcement — "X is being sold for Y, what are the bids?" From there you can simply watch: a countdown begins (60 seconds by default), and a crowd of four to eight real, named competitors — each described by how they look or who they represent, like "a lady with a pearl necklace" or "a lawyer bidding for a client", and each with their own personal spending limit — may jump in with their own bids at any moment, with the current price and time left announced periodically as it plays out.

If you'd like to join in, place a bid at any time before the clock runs out. Whoever is leading when time expires wins the item — you don't have to click anything to let the round play out on its own. Once a round ends, a short pause follows and then a brand new item automatically comes up for auction, so the Auction House keeps running for as long as you leave the screen open.

The round length can be changed right from the Auction House screen (5–120 seconds) — this applies to the next round, not the one currently in progress. Anything you win goes into your own collection on the same screen, and can be resold later at a freshly negotiated price each time.

Gambling

Feeling lucky? The in-game casino lets you risk your cash for a chance at a bigger payout. As always, the house has an edge — play for fun, not as a financial strategy.

Leaderboard

From the Settings screen you can opt in to the public leaderboard. Once you do, your username, cash balance, and current game day are shown on a shared "Top Scores" list that every player can see. Only your cash counts, so goods in your inventory or warehouse don't change your score. You can turn this off again at any time.

Message of the Day

When the game starts, it can show and read out a short message from the developer, such as news about an update. Each message is shown only once. You can read the latest message again from the main menu, or turn the automatic reading off in Settings.

Support

Found a bug, have a question, or just want to tell us something? Open the Support / Ticket screen from the main menu at any time — no separate account or website needed. You can optionally attach your recent in-game history to a ticket to help us understand what happened.

Your tickets are listed with their status. When a ticket has been closed, the list shows it as closed and, once you open it, the reply box becomes read-only with a note that you can no longer reply. If you still need help, just open a new ticket.

We'd love to support more languages. If you'd like to see the game in your own language, or you're willing to help translate it, please let us know through the Support / Ticket screen — we're very open to adding new languages with the community's help.

Settings

From the Settings screen you can change the game's language (the game remembers your choice), adjust music and sound-effect volume, turn the typing sound on or off, turn the reading of the message of the day on or off, turn the automatic update check at startup on or off (you can always check manually with the Check for Updates button), manage your leaderboard participation, and switch between Normal and Portable mode. The country for a new game (Turkey, the United States, the United Kingdom, or Australia) is chosen when you start that new game.

Portable Mode

Portable mode lets you carry the game, with all your saves and settings, on a USB drive or move it to another computer.

  1. Make sure the game's main .exe file is in its own empty folder.
  2. Open Settings and choose Switch to Portable Mode, then confirm.
  3. Your saves, settings and language choice are copied into a KaraborsaData folder next to the .exe, and a small file named portable.flag is created.
  4. Copy or move the whole game folder (the .exe together with KaraborsaData and portable.flag) to any computer and play from there.

To go back, open Settings and choose Switch to Normal Mode. Your data is copied back to this computer's normal game data folder. After switching, please close and reopen the game so everything uses the new location.

Good to know: don't delete portable.flag while you want to stay in portable mode — without it the game goes back to looking for your data in the normal location. Portable mode also can't be turned on if the game folder is write-protected (for example inside Program Files); move the game to a normal folder first.

Saving Your Progress

There is no online account: your progress is saved automatically on this device as you play, in a save file for each username. To play on another computer, use Portable Mode and copy the game folder.
