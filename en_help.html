Karaborsa Simülasyonu — Help

Welcome! Karaborsa Simülasyonu ("Black Market Simulation") is a fully fictional trading and business-management game. You buy and sell goods on a fluctuating black market, open front companies to launder your money, invest in real estate, hire employees, manage risk with an informant, and try to avoid the police — all while building the biggest fortune you can. This page is your quick reference for how everything works.

Buying & Selling

The heart of the game is the product list: every item has a price that changes from day to day, sometimes gently, sometimes sharply after a random event. Buy low, hold onto stock, and sell high. Watch the category of each product (press D to hear it) — some categories carry more risk (and more police attention) than others.

Companies

A company is a legitimate front business you can open in a city of your choice. Companies cost money to found and have a daily upkeep, but they generate their own monthly profit and — most importantly — let you launder money, turning risky cash into clean, reportable income. Miss too many upkeep payments and a company can go under.

Land

Real estate is a slower, steadier way to grow your wealth. Land prices drift over time depending on the district, and you can also take out a loan using land you own as collateral. Open F6 for the full land management screen.

Bank Loans

Short on cash? The bank will lend you money at a set interest rate, repaid over a number of daily installments. Missing installments has consequences, so only borrow what you can realistically pay back.

Employees

Hire staff to help run your operation. Employees are paid regularly and can be managed from the Employee screen (F7).

Informants & the Police

The riskier your activity, the more "police heat" you build up. Hiring an informant costs money but gives you advance warning before trouble arrives, letting you cut your losses or lay low. If your heat gets too high and you're caught, you'll end up in jail for a number of days — during which your businesses and loans keep running without you, so it pays to stay prepared.

Auction House

Press F8 to open the Auction House. This is a live, real-time auction: the moment you open it, a random item (an antique, a piece of jewelry, a classic car, and more) goes up with an opening announcement — "X is being sold for Y, what are the bids?" From there you can simply watch: a countdown begins (60 seconds by default), and real, named competitors — each with their own personal spending limit — may jump in with their own bids at any moment, with the current price and time left announced periodically as it plays out.

If you'd like to join in, place a bid at any time before the clock runs out. Whoever is leading when time expires wins the item — you don't have to click anything to let the round play out on its own. Once a round ends, a short pause follows and then a brand new item automatically comes up for auction, so the Auction House keeps running for as long as you leave the screen open.

The round length can be changed right from the Auction House screen (5–120 seconds) — this applies to the next round, not the one currently in progress. Anything you win goes into your own collection on the same screen, and can be resold later at a freshly negotiated price each time.

Gambling

Feeling lucky? The in-game casino lets you risk your cash for a chance at a bigger payout. As always, the house has an edge — play for fun, not as a financial strategy.

Leaderboard

From the Settings screen you can opt in to the public leaderboard. Once you do, your username, cash balance, and current game day are shown on a shared "Top Scores" list that every player can see. You can turn this off again at any time.

Support

Found a bug, have a question, or just want to tell us something? Open the Support / Ticket screen from the main menu at any time — no separate account or website needed. You can optionally attach your recent in-game history to a ticket to help us understand what happened.

We'd love to support more languages. If you'd like to see the game in your own language, or you're willing to help translate it, please let us know through the Support / Ticket screen — we're very open to adding new languages with the community's help.

Settings

From the Settings screen you can adjust sound and music volume, change the game's language, choose whether your country is Turkey, the United States, the United Kingdom, or Australia for a new game, turn cloud backup on or off, and manage your leaderboard participation.

Account & Saving

Your progress is tied to your account and saved automatically as you play. If your cloud backup is turned on, you can pick up where you left off on another device by logging in with the same account.

A quick reminder: everything in this game — the black market, weapons, drugs, money laundering, and crime — is entirely fictional and exists purely for entertainment. Nothing here reflects or endorses real-world illegal activity.
